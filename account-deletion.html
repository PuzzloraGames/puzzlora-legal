Fruit Drop Pop: Drop & Match

Package: com.joydream.fruitdroppop

English

The game does not allow users to create an account and does not maintain a server-side player account. Therefore, there is no app account to request deletion of.

To delete locally stored game progress and settings:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Select Apps and choose Fruit Drop Pop: Drop & Match.
  3. Select Storage, then Clear storage/data; or uninstall the game.

This action permanently removes local progress from that device. Data processed independently by Google services is subject to Google's controls and retention policies. You can review your Google privacy controls at Google Data & Privacy.

For a privacy inquiry, email tuwenlu@gmail.com and identify the game. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

简体中文

本游戏不允许用户创建账号，也不维护服务器端玩家账号，因此不存在需要申请删除的应用账号。

删除设备本地游戏进度和设置的方法：

  1. 打开Android设备的设置。
  2. 进入应用，选择 Fruit Drop Pop: Drop & Match。
  3. 进入存储并选择清除存储空间/数据，或者直接卸载游戏。

上述操作会永久删除该设备上的本地进度。由Google服务独立处理的数据受Google自身控制工具和保留政策约束。用户可以前往Google数据和隐私管理相关设置。

如有隐私请求，请发送邮件至 tuwenlu@gmail.com 并注明游戏名称。请勿发送密码、支付卡号或其他无关敏感信息。
