Puzzlora Games ("we," "us," or "our") provides the following mobile games (collectively, the "Games"):

  • Fruit Drop Pop: Drop & Match (package name: com.joydream.fruitdroppop)
  • PandaLogic: Brain Puzzle Games (package name: com.pandalogic.sudoku.brainpuzzle)

This Privacy Policy applies to all Games listed above. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Games. The Games are intended for a general audience that includes children and adults.

1. No App Account

The game does not offer account registration or sign-in, and we do not operate a server-side player account system. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data may permanently remove local progress.

2. Information Processed

We do not ask players to provide a name, email address, phone number, profile photo, chat message, or precise location. However, the game uses third-party services that may automatically process limited data:

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK provides advertising and may process device information, identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and related information.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand app usage and improve the game.
  • Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and diagnostic information to identify and fix technical problems.
  • Google Play Billing processes purchases. Google handles payment credentials under its own terms and privacy policy.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Children and Age Screening

The game uses a neutral age-screening mechanism. Users identified as children are placed in a child-appropriate experience. For those users, advertising requests are configured for age-appropriate treatment, personalized and interest-based advertising is disabled, and analytics collection is restricted or disabled as required. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal contact details or create an account. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has provided personal information may contact us at the address below.

5. Advertising and Consent

For users who are not identified as children, advertising and related data processing depend on region, applicable law, device settings, and consent choices. Where required, a consent or privacy-choice message is shown. Users can revisit available privacy choices from within the game where required.

6. Data Sharing

Data may be transmitted to the service providers identified above so they can provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payment processing, security, and fraud-prevention services. We do not sell personal information for money. Third-party providers process information under their own policies and applicable contractual restrictions.

7. Security and Retention

Information transmitted by the game and supported service providers is protected in transit using encrypted connections where supported. Local game progress remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Third-party providers retain information according to their documented retention settings and policies. We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security, and fraud prevention.

8. Your Choices and Deletion

You can remove locally stored progress by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it. Device advertising and privacy settings may provide additional controls. Because the game has no player account, there is no server-side app account to delete. For details, see our Data and Account Deletion page.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, SDKs, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: tuwenlu@gmail.com.

3. 第三方服务

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK：用于提供广告，可能处理设备信息、标识符、IP地址、广告互动及诊断信息。
  • Google Analytics for Firebase：帮助我们了解应用使用情况并改进游戏。
  • Firebase Crashlytics：处理崩溃和诊断信息，以定位和修复技术问题。

详细信息请参阅Google隐私权政策及Firebase隐私与安全说明。

4. 儿童与年龄筛选

游戏采用中立的年龄筛选机制。被识别为儿童的用户将进入适合儿童的数据处理路径：广告请求会设置适龄处理，关闭个性化和基于兴趣的广告，并根据要求限制或停用分析数据收集。我们不会故意要求儿童提交个人联系方式或创建账号。家长或监护人如认为儿童提供了个人信息，可以通过下方邮箱联系我们。

5. 广告与同意

对于未被识别为儿童的用户，广告及相关数据处理取决于所在地区、适用法律、设备设置和用户的同意选择。法律要求时，游戏会显示同意或隐私选择消息，并在需要时提供重新管理隐私选择的入口。

6. 数据共享

为了提供广告、分析、崩溃报告、支付、安全和防欺诈服务，数据可能传输给上述服务提供商。我们不会以金钱为对价出售个人信息。第三方服务提供商会按照其政策和适用合同限制处理信息。

7. 安全与保留

游戏及相关服务提供商传输的信息会在支持的情况下使用加密连接保护。本地游戏进度会保存在设备上，直到用户清除应用数据或卸载游戏。第三方服务商依据其保留设置和政策保存信息。我们仅在实现本政策用途、履行法律义务、解决争议以及保障安全和防欺诈所合理需要的期限内保留信息。

8. 用户选择与删除

用户可以通过清除游戏存储或卸载游戏删除本地进度；还可以通过设备广告和隐私设置使用其他控制。由于游戏没有玩家账号，因此不存在可删除的服务器端应用账号。详情参阅数据与账号删除说明。

9. 政策更新

当游戏功能、SDK或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并在本页面公布新版本及更新日期。
